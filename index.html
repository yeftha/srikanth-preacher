About

A servant of the Word in Guntur

Evangelist Kuchipudi Srikanth is a preacher of the Gospel based in Guntur, Andhra Pradesh. His ministry is centred on preaching God's Word plainly, praying with families, and encouraging believers to walk faithfully with Christ every day.

Whether you are seeking prayer, spiritual guidance, or someone to visit and pray in your home, you are welcome to reach out. No request is too small to bring before the Lord.
